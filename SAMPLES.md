# Bundled samples

Two families ship inside the engine, in `crates/engine/assets/samples/`: sounds cut from lofisons' own tracks, and the Dust kit from a public-domain orchestra.

## From lofisons' own tracks

| Sound | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Shadow** kit | 16 hits from the drum stems of *Shadow Ambient*, *Silent Focus*, *Void Pulse* and *Raw Frequency* |
| **Session** kit | 16 hits from the drum stems of four *Modular Code Sessions* |
| **Shadow sub**, **Silent sub**, **Session bass** | one steady note from each bass stem, played across the keys |
| **Shadow air**, **Silent air**, **Session air** | four seconds of each pad stem where the harmony holds still, played as grains |

The tracks are the author's own; these excerpts ship with the code.

They are built by [`tools/build_stem_sounds.py`](tools/build_stem_sounds.py) from the stems (which are not in this repository). For the kits it finds every hit, sorts them by where their energy sits, and keeps for each pad the most typical hit that stands alone. Kits are mono, 16-bit, 32 kHz; basses and airs 24 kHz.

## Dust kit

The kit's internal id is `dust` (older project files say `polvo`, and still open).

### Source and license

They all come from **VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (VSCO-2 CE)**, recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell (Versilian Studios), with edits by Elan Hickler (Soundemote).

- Repository: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE

### What is used and how it was processed

| Instrument in Mjolnir | Source files |
|---|---|
| **Dust kit** | Orchestral and hand percussion from `VSCO 1 Percussion/drums/` and `Percussion/`: muffled bass drum, old snare, hand hit, tambourine, tumba, conga, quinto, rim, cowbell, cymbals, guiro, snare taps and claves |

Processed with [`tools/build_samples.py`](tools/build_samples.py), which is reproducible:
- Mono, 16-bit, 24 kHz. The reduced resolution is part of the lo-fi character and the small size.
- Leading silence trimmed, tail faded, peak normalized.
- Drums: start right on the transient and an exponential decay to tighten very resonant orchestral hits. Kicks are sped up (shorter and higher) and everything goes through a 24 dB/oct low-pass (stronger on metals and tambourine) for a dusty sound.

To regenerate them (needs git, ffmpeg and numpy):

```sh
python3 tools/build_samples.py
```

## What is not a sample

Everything else (felt piano, FM electric piano, synths, the Lo-Fi, Modular, Acoustic, Boom and Club kits, the Cloud and Glass textures) is generated by synthesis in Mjolnir's own code. No third-party recordings beyond the Dust kit.
