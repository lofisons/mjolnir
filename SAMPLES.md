# Bundled samples

Two families ship inside the engine, in `crates/engine/assets/samples/`: sounds cut from lofisons' own tracks, and the Dust kit from a public-domain orchestra.

## From lofisons' own tracks

| Sound | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Shadow** kit | 16 hits from the drum stems of *Shadow Ambient*, *Silent Focus*, *Void Pulse* and *Raw Frequency* |
| **Session** kit | 16 hits from the drum stems of four *Modular Code Sessions* |
| **Shadow chops**, **Silent chops** | 16 slices of each track's pads and keys, cut where something new begins |
| **Shadow sub**, **Silent sub** | one steady note from each bass stem, played across the keys |
| **Shadow air**, **Silent air** | four seconds of each pad stem where the harmony holds still, played as grains |
| **Shadow lead**, **Silent lead** | the passages where one voice stands almost alone (the end of *Shadow Ambient*, the opening of *Silent Focus*), played as grains |

The tracks are the author's own; these excerpts ship with the code.

They are built by [`tools/build_stem_sounds.py`](tools/build_stem_sounds.py) from the stems (which are not in this repository). For the kits it finds every hit, sorts them by where their energy sits, and keeps for each pad the most typical hit that stands alone. Kits are mono, 16-bit, 32 kHz; basses, airs and leads 24 kHz; chops 22.05 kHz.

## Real instruments

Soft piano, music box, harp, strings, quiet organ, flute and contrabass are a few recorded notes each from **VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition** (CC0, see below): the upright piano played gently, a glockenspiel, the harp, the cello and viola sections, the organ's quiet manual, the flute and a bowed double bass.

[`tools/build_instruments.py`](tools/build_instruments.py) fetches only the notes it needs and builds each instrument: a low-pass darkens it, every note is tuned and levelled, and held instruments get a cross-faded loop. One mono 16-bit 22.05 kHz file per instrument, with a small JSON map of its notes beside it.

## Dust kit (no longer offered)

The kit's internal id is `dust` (older project files say `polvo`, and still open).

### What is used and how it was processed

| Instrument in Mjolnir | Source files |
|---|---|
| **Dust kit** | Orchestral and hand percussion from `VSCO 1 Percussion/drums/` and `Percussion/`: muffled bass drum, old snare, hand hit, tambourine, tumba, conga, quinto, rim, cowbell, cymbals, guiro, snare taps and claves |

Processed with [`tools/build_samples.py`](tools/build_samples.py), which is reproducible:
- Mono, 16-bit, 24 kHz. The reduced resolution is part of the lo-fi character and the small size.
- Leading silence trimmed, tail faded, peak normalized.
- Drums: start right on the transient and an exponential decay to tighten very resonant orchestral hits. Kicks are sped up (shorter and higher) and everything goes through a 24 dB/oct low-pass (stronger on metals and tambourine) for a dusty sound.

To regenerate them (needs git, ffmpeg and numpy):

```sh
python3 tools/build_samples.py
```

## What is not a sample

The tape pad and the Lo-Fi, Trap and Modular kits are generated by synthesis in Mjolnir's own code. Older synthesized sounds (the felt piano, the FM keys, the Acoustic, Boom and Club kits) and the Dust kit are no longer offered, but projects that use them still open.
