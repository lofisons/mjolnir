# Bundled samples

Mjolnir ships a small sample set (~350 KB, only the Dust kit) in `crates/engine/assets/samples/`, embedded in the binary. The kit's internal id is `dust` (older project files say `polvo`, and still open).

## Source and license

They all come from **VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (VSCO-2 CE)**, recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell (Versilian Studios), with edits by Elan Hickler (Soundemote).

- Repository: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain). They can be used, modified and redistributed without restriction, even commercially. CC0 requires no attribution; we give it anyway, with thanks.

## What is used and how it was processed

| Instrument in Mjolnir | Source files |
|---|---|
| **Dust kit** | Orchestral and hand percussion from `VSCO 1 Percussion/drums/` and `Percussion/`: muffled bass drum, old snare, hand hit, tambourine, tumba, conga, quinto, rim, cowbell, cymbals, guiro, snare taps and claves |

Processed with [`tools/build_samples.py`](tools/build_samples.py), which is reproducible:
- Mono, 16-bit, 24 kHz. The reduced resolution is part of the lo-fi character and the small size.
- Leading silence trimmed, tail faded, peak normalized.
- Drums: start right on the transient and an exponential decay to tighten very resonant orchestral hits. Kicks are sped up (shorter and higher) and everything goes through a 24 dB/oct low-pass (stronger on metals and tambourine) for a dusty sound.

To regenerate them (needs git, ffmpeg and numpy):

```sh
python3 tools/build_samples.py
```

## What is not a sample

Everything else (felt piano, FM electric piano, synths, the Lo-Fi, Modular, Acoustic, Boom and Club kits, textures) is generated by synthesis in Mjolnir's own code. No third-party recordings.
